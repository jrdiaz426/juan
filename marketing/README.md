# Sunday's Fold — launch marketing kit

Made from the Sunday's Fold brand system. Every claim here is one the business can already stand behind (pickup & delivery, wash/dry/fold, back within 24 hours of pickup, no need to be home, six cities, a real person on the phone). No prices or offers are included: add them only once they're confirmed on the website.

## Before you post

1. Put `https://sundaysfold.com/book.html` in your Instagram bio link (Edit profile → Links). Posts 1–3 say "Book at the link in bio".
2. On the story, add a Link sticker pointing to the same address, placed over or under the orange bar.
3. Upload the PNGs as they are: Instagram keeps 1080-wide images sharp. Don't add filters.

## Printing the flyer

- Print shop: send `flyer-letter-print-bleed.pdf` and ask for US Letter, trimmed, full colour, 100 lb matte or uncoated text stock.
- Before a big run, print one copy and scan the QR code with a phone to check it opens the booking page.
- For door drops, check your city's rules on leaving flyers, and never put them in mailboxes (that's reserved for USPS).
