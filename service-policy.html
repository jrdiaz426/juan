Service Policy

How Sunday's Fold handles your laundry, day to day.

This page is a draft, not a published policy. Only the items below marked "Confirmed" are backed by something actually stated elsewhere on the site. Everything marked "Needs owner input" is a placeholder — we have not invented an answer for it.

Confirmed

  • Wash preferences: tell us your preferences when you book (cold wash, no fabric softener, extra hangers, etc.) and we follow them on every order.
  • Detergent: plant-based, dye-free detergent is available at no extra cost — note it in your wash preferences.
  • Turnaround: standard orders are washed, dried, folded, and returned within 24 hours of pickup; we'll tell you before we start if a load needs longer.
  • Cancellations: you can cancel a pickup any time before it's picked up, at no charge.

Needs owner input

  • Order separation: how individual households' laundry is kept separate through washing, drying, and delivery.
  • Stains & damage: the process if an item arrives stained, gets damaged during processing, or goes missing.
  • Payment timing: whether payment is collected at booking, at pickup, or after delivery, and which payment methods are accepted.
  • Minimum order: the pricing page currently states a 10 lb minimum for the Wash & Fold plan — please confirm this is current policy (see the flagged note on the Pricing section).

Questions

For anything not covered here yet, call or text (323) 470-3462 or email ccc@sundaysfold.com.
