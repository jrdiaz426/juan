Service Policy

How our pickup and delivery service works.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Services

Laundry pickup and delivery, with wash, dry & fold.

Service area

Gardena, Lawndale, Hawthorne, Torrance, Redondo Beach, and El Segundo, California. Not sure we reach your street? Call or text (323) 470-3462.

Requesting a pickup

Send a pickup request with your preferred date and time of day. A request isn’t a booking: we contact you to confirm your pickup window first.

Pickup

Leave your bag by the door during your confirmed pickup window. You don’t need to be home.

Wash preferences

Tell us how you’d like your laundry washed when you request your pickup.

Turnaround

Your laundry comes back fresh and folded within 24 hours of pickup.

Contact

Questions? Call or text (323) 470-3462 or email ccc@sundaysfold.com.
