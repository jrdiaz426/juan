FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you pick up?

We pick up and deliver in Gardena, Lawndale, Hawthorne, Torrance, Redondo Beach, and El Segundo.

How does pickup & delivery work?

Request a pickup and we’ll confirm it with you. Once confirmed, leave your bag by the door. You don’t need to be home. We wash, dry, and fold your laundry and bring it back within 24 hours.

What’s your turnaround time?

Your laundry comes back fresh and folded within 24 hours of pickup.

How much does it cost?

Wash & Fold is $2.25 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. A Family Bag is $39 for up to 20 lbs. See Pricing for what’s included.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your bag by the door during your confirmed pickup time and we’ll take it from there.

Who do I contact about my order?

Call or text a real person at (323) 470-3462, or email ccc@sundaysfold.com.
