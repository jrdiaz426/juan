Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Who we are

Sunday's Fold provides laundry pickup and delivery (wash, dry & fold) in Gardena, Lawndale, Hawthorne, Torrance, Redondo Beach, and El Segundo, California. You can reach us at ccc@sundaysfold.com or (323) 470-3462.

Information you share with us

When you call, text, or email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, pickup address, and laundry preferences.

Our website

  • The booking and message forms on this website are not accepting submissions at this time, so information typed into them is not sent or stored.
  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.
  • The ZIP code checker runs entirely in your browser; the ZIP code you enter is not sent anywhere.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
