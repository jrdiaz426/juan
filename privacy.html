Privacy Policy

How Sunday's Fold collects, uses, and protects your information.

This page is a draft, not a published policy. Sunday's Fold has not supplied final privacy-policy text yet. Nothing on this page should be treated as a legal commitment until the owner and, ideally, a lawyer have reviewed and approved it. No data-handling guarantees are made here beyond what's factually true of the current, no-backend contact/booking forms.

What information is collected today

The current site has no backend or database. The contact and booking forms build a mailto: link and hand it to your own email app — nothing you type is sent to, or stored on, any Sunday's Fold server. Whatever you choose to send arrives as a normal email to ccc@sundaysfold.com, and from there is subject to Sunday's Fold's and your own email provider's normal handling of email.

What this policy still needs (owner input required)

Before this page can be treated as a real privacy policy, the owner needs to provide and confirm:

  • Whether/when a real booking backend or database will be added, and what customer data it will store
  • Whether any third-party services (payment processing, SMS/text notifications, analytics) are or will be used, and their own privacy practices
  • How long any collected information is retained
  • How a customer can request their information be corrected or deleted
  • A contact method specifically for privacy questions, if different from general contact

Questions

Until a final policy is published, direct any privacy questions to ccc@sundaysfold.com or (323) 470-3462.
